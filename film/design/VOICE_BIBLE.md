# Voice Bible

Each character records language, perceived age, register, pace, timbre, emotional range and pronunciation notes.
Initial languages: EN, Mandarin Chinese (Simplified subtitles), Vietnamese.
Dialogue keeps stable line IDs so voice and lip-sync can regenerate independently of image/video.
Use separate dialogue tracks from ambience/SFX. Clone/likeness use requires recorded rights metadata before publication.
Measure cross-language speaker similarity and between-character separation during MODEL-EVAL.
Acceptance: voices are intelligible, distinct, character-consistent and do not force edit timing outside the shot budget.
