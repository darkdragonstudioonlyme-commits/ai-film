# Workflow Health Records

Immutable `HEALTH_REVIEW-<workflow>-<nnn>.md` records live here.

A current workflow-health summary belongs in `PROJECT_STATE.md` only when it affects routing. Detailed symptoms/root-cause/systemic corrections stay in the immutable health review. Aggregate metrics may be updated in a reviewed health-summary record; do not turn `PROJECT_STATE.md` into a metrics log.

Retention: keep immutable health reviews that justify active policy/checker changes. Obsolete operational advice is removed from active policy and remains recoverable through these records/Git history.
