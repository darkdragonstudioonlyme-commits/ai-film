# Last Train Signal — benchmark screenplay v1

Target: 60–90 seconds. One night station scene. Original project material.

Rain lashes a nearly empty station at 23:40. AN, 28, hurries onto the platform. LINH, 31, is closing the desk.

AN produces a small red paper crane. “Does the 11:40 still stop here?”

The station lights die. The signal outside goes black. LINH raises her flashlight. “Only if we bring the signal back.”

At the service panel, AN helps free a jammed cover. Metal catches his left sleeve and cuts his forearm. LINH wraps the cut with white gauze.

She notices the crane. “You came back for this station?”

AN watches the dead signal. “For a promise.”

The signal turns green. Train headlights appear through the rain. LINH keeps the paper crane while AN boards, the fresh white bandage clearly visible.

Cut on the train pulling away and the green signal reflected in rain.
